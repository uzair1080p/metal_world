§ 01 Thesis

A tonne of copper in Rotterdam and a tonne of copper a few thousand miles east are chemically identical and commercially different. Freight, duties, currency, timing and local demand open a gap between them. We live inside that gap.

Buy where it’s abundant

Yards, mills, demolition contractors and recyclers in mature economies generate more metal than their home markets can absorb. We are their outlet.

Place where it’s needed

Fast-growing industrial bases melt, roll and cast faster than they can source domestically. We are their supply line.

Own the risk in between

We trade as principal. Price, freight, quality and FX exposure sit on our book, not our counterparties’.

§ 03 Method

Four moves between
a yard and a furnace.

  1. 01

    Source

    Direct relationships with yards, processors and generators. We quote firm, we lift on time, and we pay the way we said we would.

    → Firm bid within 24h
  2. 02

    Verify

    Every lot is inspected at loading. Photographs, weights, grade and radiation checks, with independent surveyors where the contract calls for it.

    → Pre-shipment inspection
  3. 03

    Move

    Containers and breakbulk, booked and tracked by our own logistics desk. Documentation is prepared to clear on arrival, not after it.

    → Door to discharge port
  4. 04

    Settle

    Letters of credit, CAD and structured terms through established banks. Clean paper, predictable cash cycles, no surprises.

    → LC / CAD / TT
